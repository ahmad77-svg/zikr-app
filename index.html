1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application ذِكر ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

The App is designed to provide Islamic adhkar, audio playback, reminders, favorites, progress tracking, home-screen features, and shared dhikr campaigns.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create a personal account using your name, email address, or phone number.

Some technical and usage information may nevertheless be processed by third-party services used by the App.

3. Anonymous Accounts and Campaigns

The App uses Supabase to support features such as shared dhikr campaigns. An anonymous user identifier may be created automatically so that the App can associate campaign participation and counters with a device or anonymous user.

Campaign-related information may include campaign names, selected dhikr, campaign targets, membership information, anonymous user identifiers, and dhikr count totals.

We do not require your real name, email address, or phone number to use these campaign features.
